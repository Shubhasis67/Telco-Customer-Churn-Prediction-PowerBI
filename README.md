## 📊 Buisness Insights & Key Findings

### 🔍 Core Discoveries
* **Customer Churn Dynamics:** Customer churn varies significantly across contract types, with shorter-term customers requiring particular attention.
* **Strategic Segmentation:** Tenure is an important segmentation variable, allowing the buisness to identify early-stage customers who may need stronger engagement.

### 💡Data-Driven Solution
* **Predictive Risk Modeling:** The Logistic Regression model converts customer characteristics into a churn probability, allowing customers to be prioritized based on estimated risk.
* **Executive Decision Making:** The Power BI dashboard enables management to filter high-risk customers by contract, tenure, service type, and payment method.

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations & Next Steps

Based on the insights generated by the model and dashboard, the business should implement the following targeted actions:

### 1. High-Risk Customer Retention
* **Action:** Launch proactive engagement campaigns targeting short-term contract holders before their monthly cycle ends. 
* **Incentive:** Offer loyalty discounts or exclusive perks to transition month-to-month users into longer-term contracts.

### 2. Early-Tenure Engagement Program
* **Action:** Establish an automated onboarding check-in sequence for customers in their first 3–6 months.
* **Goal:** Increase early satisfaction and product adoption to reduce early-stage churn signals identified during segmentation.

### 3. Deploy Predictive Interventions
* **Action:** Integrate the Logistic Regression model's churn probabilities into the active CRM workflow.
* **Goal:** Flag high-risk accounts automatically so account managers can prioritize customer service outreach based on estimated risk scores.

### 4. Operational Dashboard Training
* **Action:** Conduct training sessions for the management and operations teams on filtering the Power BI dashboard.
* **Goal:** Enable team leaders to independently monitor risk by contract, tenure, and payment methods to adjust regional strategies on the fly.
